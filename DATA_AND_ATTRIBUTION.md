# Data, attribution, and scope

This portfolio presents Arel Akaunu's MSc coursework. Original reports retain their citations and AI-use disclosures. Portfolio documentation and small code corrections were prepared with AI assistance; the record of edits is in EDITS.md and source mappings are in provenance.json.

## Included and omitted

| Material | Decision |
|---|---|
| Activity recognition notebooks and report | Included, with historical results labelled and missing datasets documented |
| Reddit code | Included after credential removal; no user posts, raw CSVs, or screenshots of posts republished |
| Compact MCMC notebook | Included with limited documented corrections; the larger overlapping exploratory notebook and presentation omitted |
| R test-power simulation | Included; standalone synthetic-data study |
| Dissertation | Included as report and aggregate figures; dataset preparation credited to SquashLevels |
| Multivariate analysis report | Included as a report-based case study |
| Assignment briefs, exams, syllabi, module pages | Omitted; these are teaching/assessment materials, not portfolio projects |
| Research pitch and general topic essay | Omitted in favour of completed analytical work |
| R raffle/calorie exercises and submersible analysis | Omitted to keep the selection focused; these require additional statistical review, and the supplied data have no redistribution licence in the archive |
| Competition predictions | Omitted; not useful without the associated data and evaluation |
| University LaTeX templates, logos, acknowledgements | Omitted from source publication; the original thesis remains as a report |

No raw datasets are redistributed. The missing HAR and squash data are not fabricated or replaced. Python dependency lists describe imports; they are not verified historical lockfiles.

## Reuse

No blanket open-source licence has been applied to the coursework, reports, or third-party material. Existing citations and the rights of their respective owners remain relevant. Contact the author about reuse.
